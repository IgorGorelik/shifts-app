# Shifts — public pages

The about page, privacy policy and terms of use of **Shifts** (משמרות · Смены), an iPhone and Apple Watch app that shows a volunteer team's shift roster from a Google Sheet and keeps your shift events in Google Calendar.

Published with GitHub Pages at <https://igorgorelik.github.io/shifts-app/>:

- About — <https://igorgorelik.github.io/shifts-app/>
- Privacy Policy — <https://igorgorelik.github.io/shifts-app/privacy.html>
- Terms of Use — <https://igorgorelik.github.io/shifts-app/terms.html>

Each page is in English, Hebrew and Russian. The texts live in `tools/build.py`, which writes the three HTML files (`python3 tools/build.py`); `site.css`, `site.js` and the icons are edited by hand.

Questions or problems: [open an issue](https://github.com/IgorGorelik/shifts-app/issues).
